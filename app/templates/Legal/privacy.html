Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 2026  ·  Effective: August 2026
AdvisorNest is committed to protecting the privacy of financial advisors and their clients. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. We do not sell personal data to third parties.
1. Who We Are

AdvisorNest is a software-as-a-service platform providing AI-powered portfolio recommendation tools for licensed financial advisors. References to "AdvisorNest", "we", "us", or "our" in this policy refer to AdvisorNest and its operators.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:

Category Examples Purpose
Advisor Account Data Name, email, firm name, CRD number Account creation and PDF reports
Client Profile Data Client name, age, risk tolerance, investment amount Generating portfolio recommendations
Usage Data Login times, features used, recommendations generated Service improvement and security
AI Interaction Data Prompts sent to AI, recommendations generated Generating recommendations, audit trail
Meeting Notes Advisor-entered notes about client meetings Practice management
3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the AdvisorNest platform
  • Generate AI-powered portfolio recommendations
  • Create and store client profiles and recommendations
  • Generate PDF reports for advisor use
  • Authenticate your account and maintain security
  • Send transactional emails (account verification, password reset)
  • Improve the Service based on usage patterns
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
4. AI and Third Party Services

AdvisorNest uses the following third-party services to operate the platform:

  • OpenAI — Portfolio recommendations and suitability notes are generated using OpenAI's GPT models. Client profile data is sent to OpenAI's API to generate recommendations. OpenAI's privacy policy applies to this data processing.
  • Supabase — We use Supabase to store advisor accounts, client profiles, and recommendations. Data is stored in secure, encrypted databases.
  • FRED (Federal Reserve) — We fetch publicly available market data including Treasury yields and interest rates. No personal data is shared with FRED.

We do not share personal data with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Client Data

As a financial advisor using AdvisorNest, you are the data controller for your clients' personal information. You are responsible for:

  • Obtaining appropriate consent from clients before entering their data
  • Complying with applicable privacy laws regarding client data
  • Maintaining appropriate data security practices
  • Notifying clients if their data is processed by AI systems

AdvisorNest acts as a data processor for client personal information entered by advisors. We process this data only as instructed by advisors through use of the Service.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/HTTPS
  • Encryption of data at rest in our database
  • Row-level security ensuring advisors can only access their own data
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth
  • Regular security reviews and updates
7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory compliance purposes.

Recommendation and client data may be retained for up to 7 years to support regulatory compliance and audit requirements applicable to financial advisory businesses.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability — Request your data in a portable format
  • Objection — Object to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@advisornest.com

9. Cookies

AdvisorNest uses session cookies to maintain your login state. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may prevent you from logging in to the Service.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or a prominent notice on the Service. Your continued use of AdvisorNest after notification constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Privacy Questions or Concerns?

Contact our privacy team at privacy@advisornest.com
