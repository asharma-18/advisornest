What it does

Everything from draft to documentation, in one place.

AI-Assisted Recommendations

Generate risk-tiered portfolio options grounded in real market data — P/E ratios, dividend yields, and current pricing — not static model templates.

Suitability Documentation

Every recommendation ships with a formal suitability note, generated automatically and kept in sync whenever you revise the allocation.

Portfolio Drift Monitoring

Track how each client's actual holdings drift from target allocation over time, with rebalancing flags surfaced automatically.

Client-Ready PDF Reports

Download a polished, print-ready report — allocation breakdown, instrument reasoning, and suitability note — in one click.

Data & Security

Your client data stays yours.

Encrypted storage

Client records and recommendations are stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL), encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit via TLS.

No data resale

Client and portfolio data is never sold, shared, or used to train models outside your account.

You control access

Only your logged-in account can see your clients' data — there's no cross-advisor visibility.
